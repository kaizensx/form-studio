# Code of Conduct

Form Studio is built by people who make physical things, and it welcomes everyone who wants to: beginners and
engineers, hobbyists and professionals, of any background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to CAD, printing or open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Critique designs, code and ideas, never the person who made them.
- Credit other people's work, recipes and measurements.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the next person who reads them.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, discussions or reviews.
- Anything else a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in the repository, issues, discussions, pull requests and any space that represents the project.
Report a concern privately to the maintainers through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/kaizensx/form-studio/security/advisories/new) (choose it for conduct
reports too) or by contacting [@kaizensx](https://github.com/kaizensx) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants, in proportion to the behaviour.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.
